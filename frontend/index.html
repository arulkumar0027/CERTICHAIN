How it works

Three steps.
Zero trust required.

Issue

Each field is salted and hashed, then combined into one fingerprint. Only that fingerprint is written to the blockchain.

Share

The holder picks which fields to reveal and shares a link, a QR code or a file.

Verify

Anyone recomputes the fingerprint and checks it against the contract. Genuine, revoked or altered, in a second.
